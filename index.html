The work

Two are live. One is still being built.

01 · Live · Guitar

ChordXP

Jam with a live band in your browser.

Explore chords and scales on the fretboard, including alternate tunings. Then open Studio and write chords, melody, lyrics, and rhythm while a virtual backing band — guitars, piano, bass, and drums — follows the song. Print the guitar tab when you want it on a stand. Free in the browser, no account required.

Open chordxp.com (opens in a new tab) →

02 · Live · Trades

MyNuQuote

Clear estimates. Easy pay.

Quoting software for contractors and tradespeople, in the same lane as tools like Jobber. Write an estimate, send a pay link, and the customer accepts and pays a deposit or the full amount by card or Venmo. You can see what is paid and what is still open. MyNuQuote doesn’t take a cut of the payment.

Open mynuquote.com (opens in a new tab) →

03 · In progress · Desktop

j-tunes

A podcast library that lives on your machine.

A desktop podcast player and manager, still in progress, for a large local library. Subscribe from an RSS or Atom feed, or search the podcast directory. Download episodes, keep an Up Next queue, and search what you already have — no account. The repo is public while it is being built.
